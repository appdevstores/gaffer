Common questions

Is my data in the cloud?

No. Everything (seasons, rosters, matches) is stored only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers.

How do I clear old matches?

Open a season → Storage Management → select matches → Delete. You can also delete an entire season from the same screen.

How do the rotation meters work?

Gaffer calculates an equal-playing-time target for the match. Each player's battery drains as they approach it — green is fresh, red means they've hit their fair share and could come off.

Can I email a match report?

Yes — at full time, tap “Email Match Report” and the summary is composed in your device's mail app using your own email address.
